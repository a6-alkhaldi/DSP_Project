We collect information you provide, such as your name, email, and recipes, as well as usage data (e.g., browsing activity) to improve our services.

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with trusted service providers (e.g., email services) to operate the platform, or as required by law.

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal data. Contact us at support@SmartMeal.com to exercise these rights.
